First time with us?

Tap a question.

Do I need to sign up first?

No. Come to any run and say hello. Please sign the waiver before your third run.

What if I fall behind?

You won’t be alone. The easy group is no-drop and every group has someone running at the back.

Is there a bag drop?

Yes, at the meeting point. Keep phones and valuables on you.

Where do we go after?

On Saturdays we usually end with coffee and breakfast near the start. Ask a leader.

What should I bring?

Water, a light jacket for 6 AM, and reflective gear for the evening runs.

Run safe

  • Run facing traffic where there’s no pavement, and single file on busy roads.
  • Boda bodas and matatus stop without warning. Never assume a driver saw you.
  • One earbud at most, low volume. Better: none in a group.
  • Dogs and strollers are welcome on Saturdays. Tell the leader and stay at the back.
  • Lightning or heavy rain cancels the run. We post it in WhatsApp.
  • Wear bright or reflective gear in the evening and carry a phone.
  • If someone stops, someone stops with them.
