How a run goes

Nervous about your first one? This is all it is.

1

Meet up

Arrive about 10 minutes early. Say hi, drop your bag, find your group.

2

Warm up

A few minutes of easy jogging and stretches, all together.

3

Run

Go at your own pace with your group. Chatting is encouraged.

4

Cool down

Stretch, take photos, and head for coffee and breakfast.
